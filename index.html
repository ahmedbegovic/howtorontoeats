Restaurants mentioned on r/FoodToronto

I ran every post and comment on r/FoodToronto from January 2025 to September 2026 through a language model and had it pull out the restaurants people mentioned. I wanted to see which ones keep getting recommended, and which neighbourhoods hardly come up.

Restaurants by cuisine

Google lists a cuisine for of the restaurants on the map. It files some of them under a dish instead, like sushi, ramen or pizza. I moved those to the cuisine the dish belongs to, so a sushi place counts as Japanese and a pizza place as Italian.

Hidden gems and pushback

Every comment gets a number from 0 to 100 based on how its upvotes compare with the other comments in the same thread, where 100 is the most upvoted. A hidden gem is a place with 10 to 60 mentions, at least 85% of them positive, where the positive comments are also highly upvoted. A place is getting pushback when the comments criticising it are more upvoted than the ones praising it.

Method

Data
Every r/FoodToronto post and comment from January 2025 to September 2026 (10,878 posts, 218,926 comments), filtered out of 42 monthly Reddit dumps from Arctic Shift (about 1.3 TB compressed, 7.9 billion records).
Extraction
A language model read each thread and listed every restaurant mentioned, with a location hint and a sentiment (+1, 0 or −1). I tried keyword matching first and it missed too much.
Sentiment
A mention is positive when the comment praises the place or recommends it. Suggesting a place when someone asks for recommendations counts as positive, and so does agreeing with someone else's recommendation ("+1", "this"). It's neutral when the place comes up without an opinion, as in a question, a passing mention or a plain fact. It's negative when the comment criticises the place or warns people off it. Each mention is judged from its own comment only. A comment that mixes praise and criticism still gets one label, whichever way the model read it.
Accuracy
A second language model read 300 random posts and comments without seeing the first one's answers, and I then checked its labels by hand. Of the mentions the pipeline found, 93.7% were also in that second reading (95% interval 89.9 to 96.1). Of the mentions in the second reading, the pipeline had 91.5% (87.4 to 94.3). Where both found a mention, they gave it the same sentiment 91.9% of the time (87.8 to 94.8). Neutral is the weak spot: the pipeline called 12 of 55 neutral mentions positive.
Restaurants
I searched each name in Google Places and had a model pick the right match. Names Google didn't find were then looked up by exact name in the City of Toronto's DineSafe inspection records. The count is every location I could place inside the GTA, with each branch counted where it stands. It leaves out places outside the GTA, big chains and grocery stores, names with no address, and names mentioned only once or twice.
Map
Toronto neighbourhood boundaries are from City of Toronto Open Data (Open Government Licence - Toronto), and the other GTA municipalities from OpenStreetMap. Each restaurant is counted in the area its Google coordinates fall inside. Whether a place is closed is Google's call, as of October 2026.
